Wallgates Sensors bundles the following open-source components:

- LibreHardwareMonitorLib — MPL-2.0 — https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor
- DiskInfoToolkit — MPL-2.0 — https://github.com/Blacktempel/DiskInfoToolkit
- RAMSPDToolkit-NDD — MPL-2.0 — https://github.com/Blacktempel/RAMSPDToolkit
- BlackSharp.Core — MPL-2.0 — https://github.com/Blacktempel/BlackSharp
- HidSharp — Apache-2.0 — https://www.zer7.com/software/hidsharp
- System.Memory, System.Buffers and related .NET packages — MIT — https://github.com/dotnet

Source code of the MPL-2.0 components is available at the URLs above (MPL-2.0, section 3.2).
The PawnIO driver (https://pawnio.eu, by namazso) is not bundled: when the user enables advanced
sensors, Wallgates downloads the official signed installer from
https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Setup and verifies its Authenticode signature before running it.
